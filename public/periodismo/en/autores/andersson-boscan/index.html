Andersson Boscán

Also known as: Boscán · Andersson Boscan · Andersson Boscán Pico

Ecuadorian investigative journalist. He co-founded La Posta in 2018 and ran it until December 2025; he led El Gran Padrino, the investigation that brought President Guillermo Lasso to an impeachment trial. Since 2023 he has published from exile in Toronto, Canada, and hosts Boscán & La Moni with Mónica Velásquez.

Almost everything in the Boscán & La Moni archive started with a phone call. The people who come to him are the ones who are fed up: the agents who handed over the Senain's organizational chart, the CELEC technicians who recorded their bosses in a Houston hotel, the witness who recorded Judiciary Council members in a judge's office.

His first trade was print journalism: at Diario Expreso he published La Mancha Dorada, seven installments on the mercury poisoning the rivers of the south, and Caminosca, the private arbitration in the United States in which the sitting vice president appeared under an alias, Vidrio.

In 2018 he co-founded La Posta and ran it until December 2025. That period produced the VIP vaccinations story, which cost the Health Minister his job; Bribery Division, with the ICIJ consortium; and El Gran Padrino, thirty-four installments on how President Lasso's brother-in-law parceled out state-owned companies. For the first time in nearly a century, an Ecuadorian president faced an impeachment trial for corruption.

He left the country in 2023, after threats documented by the Committee to Protect Journalists. He keeps publishing from exile, co-founded the Red de Periodistas Libres (Free Journalists Network) and today hosts Boscán & La Moni.
